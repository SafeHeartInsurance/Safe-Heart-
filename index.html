Share your story

Your story is private. It goes only to the Safe Heart team and is never posted automatically.

  • You can leave your name off.
  • There’s a separate, optional box if you’re open to Safe Heart contacting you about sharing your story. Leave it unchecked and no one will reach out about it.
  • Please leave out other people’s full names, addresses, and other private details.

Where your answers go

Claims
Go to Safe Heart’s private Jotform account. Only the Safe Heart team can see them.
Show sign-ups and stories
Go to Safe Heart’s private Google Forms. Only the Safe Heart team can see them. They are not public and are not shown in this app.
This app
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no cookies. The app doesn’t save anything you type. The forms are run by Jotform and Google, and their privacy policies apply to what you send through them.

Safe Heart isn’t a crisis service. If you’re in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US).

Add Safe Heart to your phone

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
